## 2026-06-09 - Prevent Unbounded Navigation Stacks
**Learning:** Found multiple instances of `Navigator.pushNamed(context, '/dashbord')` in BottomNavigationBar onTap handlers and the post-login Welcome Screen. Since BottomNavigationBar switching is effectively root-level navigation, using `pushNamed` repeatedly creates an infinitely growing navigation stack, leading to memory leaks and slow tab switching.
**Action:** Replace root-level `pushNamed` tab switches with `pushReplacementNamed` to keep the stack flat while preserving back-button behavior. For un-returnable flows like post-login, use `pushNamedAndRemoveUntil(..., (route) => false)` to clear the stack entirely. Avoid `pushNamedAndRemoveUntil` for standard tab navigation, as it destroys the stack and breaks the Android back button.
